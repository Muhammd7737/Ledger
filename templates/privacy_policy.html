Privacy Policy

Last updated September 5, 2026

1. What we collect

To run your account and your board, Ledger stores:

  • Account info — your email address, username, and (if you sign in with a password) a securely hashed password. We never store passwords in plain text.
  • Google account info — if you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile info from Google to create and log you into your account.
  • Application data — the companies, roles, statuses, dates, notes, salaries, and links you enter into your board.
  • Profile picture — if you upload one.

2. How we use it

Your data is used only to operate Ledger: authenticating you, displaying your board, calculating your stats, and generating the CSV export you request. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Ledger relies on a small number of infrastructure providers to function:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication (including password hashing and Google OAuth).
  • Google — provides Google Sign-In if you choose to use it.

These providers process data on our behalf and don't use it for their own purposes. We don't share your data with anyone else.

4. Cookies & sessions

Ledger uses a session cookie to keep you logged in. It's required for the app to work and isn't used for tracking or advertising.

5. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. Archived applications are kept (not deleted) so you can restore them, until you delete your account.

6. Your rights

You can export your full application history as a CSV at any time from your dashboard. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from your profile page — this action is immediate and cannot be undone.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out through the contact details on our landing page.

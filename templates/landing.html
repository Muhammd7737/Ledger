Why Ledger

I kept losing track, so I built the tracker.

Somewhere around application thirty, the spreadsheet stopped working. Half the rows were out of date, I couldn't remember which recruiter I'd already followed up with, and a deadline slipped past me because it was buried in an email I'd archived.

Ledger is the tool I actually wanted: a board where a company's status is always current, deadlines can't hide, and checking "how's the search going" takes one glance instead of a scroll through old emails.

Questions

Before you open an account.

Is Ledger free to use? +

Yes. Creating an account, tracking applications, and exporting your data all cost nothing.

Can I sign in with Google? +

Yes, or register with a username, email, and password. Either way, your password is never stored in plain text.

Can I get my data out if I stop using it? +

Anytime. Export your full application history as a CSV directly from your dashboard.

Does it work on my phone? +

Yes — the board switches to a swipeable, single-column view on smaller screens so you can check on applications from anywhere.

Can I delete my account later? +

Yes, permanently, from your profile page — it removes your applications and account in one step.
